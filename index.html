Our Story

Authentic Flavors, Beautiful Ambiance

Welcome to Grand Spicy, Patna's premier destination for authentic North Indian and rich Mughlai cuisine. Located near the historic Bal Lila Gurudwara, we pride ourselves on serving dishes that are as rich in history as they are in flavor.

Our chefs use traditional recipes and the freshest ingredients to create mouth-watering curries, perfectly charred tandoori delicacies, and aromatic biryanis. Pair your meal with our beautiful decor for an unforgettable dining experience.
